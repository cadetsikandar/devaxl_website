## Product Overview

**One-liner:** An AI-native product studio that designs, builds, and scales SaaS and AI products for founders and CTOs.

**What it does:** Devaxl embeds a single senior squad — design, engineering, and product — inside a client's org and ships to production. Three shapes of work: taking an idea to a revenue-ready v1, modernizing a slow or brittle platform already serving users, and running a long-term embedded product team. AI is built into the products where it earns its place (RAG, agents, LLM pipelines, evals and guardrails) and used intensively across delivery so a small senior team ships like a much larger one.

**Product category:** Product engineering / software development agency. The specific shelf being contested is **"AI product studio"** — a narrower, higher-trust shelf than "software development agency", where the competition is dev shops and offshore outsourcing.

**Product type:** Service (project + retainer).

**Business model:** Three engagement models, none with public prices — MVP Build (fixed scope, fixed timeline), Dedicated Team (embedded squad, monthly), Modernization Retainer (ongoing program). Sold through a scoping call → fixed-fee discovery → written plan.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** The gap between knowing what to build and having a team that can build it to production standard, fast.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Freelancers / marketplaces** — cheap and fast to start, but no design, no PM, no architecture; the founder becomes the integrator.
- **Offshore body-shops** — sold as senior, staffed as junior; account managers between you and the people writing code.
- **Hiring in-house** — 3–6 months to a productive team, and the wrong first hire sets the architecture badly for years.
- **Premium US/EU agencies** — the quality is there, and so is a 3–5x price and a 6-week start date. `[VERIFY]` how Devaxl actually prices against these.

**What it costs them:** Missed market windows, burned runway on a v1 that has to be rewritten, and a codebase the next team refuses to touch.

**Emotional tension:** Founders fear paying twice for the same product. CTOs fear owning someone else's mess.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- **AI on both sides of the line** — AI built into the client's product *and* used across Devaxl's own delivery. Most agencies claim one; this claim is the site's sharpest asset.
- **One senior squad, no layers** — the people on the kickoff call ship the code.
- **A written plan before you commit** — fixed-fee discovery returns scope, timeline, and team; no open-ended retainer to find out what you're buying.
- **Full IP ownership from day one** — your repos, your cloud, no reused code.
- **Weekly demoable increments** — progress you can click, not a status report.

**Why customers choose us:** Speed of a small team with the discipline of a real product org, and the risk removed from the first cheque via fixed-fee discovery.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** A stalled build, a v1 that has to be thrown away, a platform too slow to sell, an engineering hire that won't land.
**Pull:** A live product in weeks, senior people, a written plan, code they own.
**Habit:** Sticking with the current freelancer because switching means re-explaining everything.
**Anxiety:** Paying twice. Losing control of the codebase. Being handed juniors after a senior sales call.
